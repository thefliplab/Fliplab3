Flip Lab Private Lesson Cancellation Policy

We value the time and commitment of our athletes and families, as well as the time of our coaches. To ensure fairness and consistency, the following cancellation policy is in effect for all private lessons:

Advance Notice
All cancellations must be made at least 24 hours in advance of the scheduled lesson. We understand that emergencies sometimes happen. In such cases, circumstances will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis at the discretion of your coach.

Late Cancellations & No-Shows
Any cancellation made with less than 24 hours notice, or failure to attend a scheduled lesson, will result in the full lesson fee being charged, unless otherwise approved under the emergency exception.

Coach Cancellations
If your coach must cancel a lesson, no fee will be charged. Since lessons are pay-as-you-go, no makeup is required, and we will simply see you at your next scheduled lesson.

Payment Terms
Lessons are considered pay-as-you-go unless otherwise arranged in writing. Payment is required at the time of service.

Repeated Violations
Consistent late cancellations or no-shows may result in the suspension or loss of private lesson privileges.

By scheduling a private lesson, the client acknowledges and agrees to follow this cancellation policy.
